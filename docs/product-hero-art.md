# Black Drink homepage artwork

The serving photograph was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and exported as 640, 1024 and 1536 pixel WebP files under `holy-elixir/assets/images/campaign/`. The product layer uses the supplied `holy-elixir/assets/images/lifestyle-v2/black-drink-actual.webp` photograph. The hero masks its background with an SVG outline so the packaging pixels and label remain intact.

## Image generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create one photorealistic high-end beverage campaign photo for the Zubrex Black Drink website, 3:2 landscape, bright white daylight ambience. Single clear short heavy-bottomed tumbler on warm white honed limestone, filled with almost black non-carbonated drink, translucent deep amber only at the sunlit edges. Three or four natural clear ice cubes, distinct cold condensation beads, a few tiny water drops and a faint wet ring on the pale counter. Frame the entire glass, center at about 66% of image width, rim near 33% of image height, base at about 84%. Keep the area around 30% of the width empty from the top to the countertop at 85% height. We will place the real tall black product package there separately, so leave room for it and keep the countertop unobstructed.
Bright clean ivory architectural studio with a softly blurred limewash wall, warm white limestone countertop, and only a hint of pale sage reflected at the far right. Full broad softbox daylight from upper left; faint shadows fall down and to the right. Think sunlit editorial drink photography with bright airy highlights, fine texture, real cold ice refraction, a clear silhouette around the glass and deep tones only inside the drink. The countertop, wall and surrounding space must stay light, cream or white, including every corner. Calm upscale composition, one glass only, eye-level product camera with a very slight look down at the ice. No extra props, pack, lettering, labels, logo, watermark, decorative fruit, mint, straw, soda fizz, people, dark background, strong color cast or holiday details.
