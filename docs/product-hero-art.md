## Image generation prompt

Use case: compositing. Make one convincing 3:2 landscape editorial campaign photograph for the Zubrex Black Drink homepage. Image 1 is the actual Zubrex package and is the exact packaging reference. Image 2 is the scene to edit and the lighting, lens, white limestone and cream palette reference. Rebuild it as one coherent photo with the Zubrex pack upright on the counter left of the glass, roughly x=32%, and the glass to the right roughly x=65%. Show the entire pack large enough for the name to read on a website. Add a single natural human hand from the upper right, in a pale sage or ivory linen sleeve, lightly grasping the right side of the glass as if reaching to take a sip. The base of the glass still rests on the stone. Keep the gesture candid and understated; don't hide the front of the glass. A subtle liquid ripple and fine condensation add movement. The drink is near black, with only a narrow dark amber glint in the sunlight, still and non-carbonated. Remove the conspicuous ice so this shows the drink on its own, and add no garnish.
Preserve the actual container from Image 1: long glossy black pack with silver pull-tab top, broad tapered shoulder, narrow black neck and the distinctive sculpted PET feet, same tall silhouette and proportions. Preserve the full black label layout and artwork at its existing proportions. Legible front text as photographed: gold and white “ZUBREX” wordmark, white vertical “BLACK DRINK”, gold vertical “Non Carbonated Water Based Beverage”, small gold “8+ pH”, and white “Net Qty: 500 ml”. Keep the green vegetarian square. No redesign, no invented certification, no added label claims, no editorial text, no other container. Keep the pack upright and front-facing so the supplied label stays recognizable.
Critical realism: the pack and glass share the same camera perspective, white balance, upper-left natural sunlight, subtle beige bounce, depth of field and contact with the white limestone. Match the highlights and specular falloff on the silver lid and glossy shoulders to the glass. Paint in realistic matching shadows falling toward lower right, especially a tight contact shadow immediately under the pack's feet that transitions into a softly tapered cast shadow. They must read as objects photographed together, with no mask halo, no floating product, no generic CGI. Ivory-white surrounding space, gentle sunlit foliage blur at the edge, and one simple living gesture create a bright, energetic, premium atmosphere. No dark background, studio lettering, sparkles, health cues or symbols.
