The Zubrex difference

A signature
all its own.

The dark pour. The black-and-gold pack. The thoughtful setting. Discover the details that give Black Drink its distinctive presence.

01 / Visual character

A study
in contrast.

Black Drink brings depth to a light setting. In clear glass, the dark pour feels sculptural; beside white linen or pale stone, it becomes a natural focal point.

The signature pack continues the story in black, gold and silver. A recognisable wordmark and vertical label create an identity designed to be seen.

Explore the signature pack↗

02 / The experience

Premium, in the small things.

01

A distinctive presentation

A dark beverage and a signature pack bring a particular character to the counter, the table and the occasion.

02

A non-carbonated format

Black Drink is a non-carbonated water-based beverage. Choose your glassware and serve according to the current pack directions.

03

A personal ritual

A quiet moment at home, a shared lunch or a welcome for guests. The setting is yours to create.

03 / A place in your day

The occasion
is what you make it.

A beautifully considered drink experience need not be elaborate. Natural light, a favourite glass and a little time can change the way an everyday pause feels.

For restaurants, hotels and events, that same attention to the details becomes part of the welcome.
