A few useful details

Before the first sip.

Is Black Drink the same as alkaline water?+

They are separate product families. Black Drink is described on the supplied pack as a non-carbonated water based beverage. Read each product’s label for its own composition and directions.

Where can I find the full ingredient list?+

The current physical pack is the best place to check ingredients and nutrition. If you need a clearer label image or a particular product detail, ask our team before ordering.

Can I order for a restaurant or event?+

Yes, you can make a hospitality or event enquiry. Share the venue, city, guest count and date. Availability, quantity and commercial terms need to be confirmed by the team.
