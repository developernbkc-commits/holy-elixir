Frequently asked

Black Drink

What is Zubrex Black Drink?+

Zubrex Black Drink is a non-carbonated water-based beverage. The supplied signature pack shows a 500 ml net quantity. Visit Black Drink for the product story and actual pack.

What makes the presentation distinctive?+

A dark pour, black-and-gold pack and silver pull-tab lid form the Zubrex signature. Discover the design and experience behind it.

Where can I check composition and nutrition?+

Read the current product label or request a clear label image from the team before ordering. The pack notes explain where to look.

How should I store and serve it?+

Follow the current pack directions, including after opening. Our serving notes offer ideas for glassware, presentation and hosting.

Availability & enquiries

Can I order on this website?+

The site helps you discover Black Drink and contact the team. There is no online checkout. Price, availability and order arrangements are confirmed directly before a purchase.

Is Black Drink available in my city?+

Share your city or postcode, expected quantity and preferred date in an enquiry. The team will confirm current coverage and delivery arrangements.

Can I ask for regular supply?+

Yes. Share your location, expected quantity and preferred frequency. Supply schedules and commercial terms are discussed directly with the team.

What happens when I use the enquiry form?+

The form prepares a draft in your email app. You review and send the email yourself. You can also write directly to Info@holy-elixir.com.
