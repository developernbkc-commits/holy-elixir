Questions & Answers

Good Questions. Clear Answers.

Product details, storage, ordering and choosing a home ionizer. Start here.

What is Holy Elixir Black Drink?+

Black Drink is a beverage with fulvic acid and trace minerals. It is a distinct product in our collection, alongside Zubrex alkaline water and home ionizers. Refer to the pack for ingredients and nutrition information.

How is Black Drink different from alkaline water?+

Black Drink has its own formulation and distinctive dark appearance. Alkaline water is described by its pH profile. Compare the individual product labels rather than assuming the two products have the same ingredients or specifications.

Where can I find ingredients, nutrition and serving information?+

Check the label on your specific pack for the full ingredient list, nutrition information, serving guidance and best-before date. Contact our team if you need current product details before ordering.

Does alkaline water offer special health benefits?+

Alkaline describes a pH above 7. We offer our beverages for refreshment and do not claim they treat illness, change blood pH, detox the body or boost immunity. For an independent overview, see Mayo Clinic’s explanation.

How should I store the beverages?+

Follow the storage instructions and best-before date on the pack. Keep the container clean, avoid direct sunlight and excessive heat, and follow the label’s guidance after opening.

How do I choose a home ionizer?+

Tell us about your source water, available counter space and usage. We can share model specifications, installation requirements and maintenance information, and arrange a demonstration.

Are all ionizer settings suitable for drinking?+

Use only the drinking-water modes specified in your model’s instructions. Other settings may be intended for separate uses. Follow the manufacturer’s guidance rather than choosing a setting solely by its pH number.

How can I order or request pricing?+

Visit our contact page to email or call the team. Please mention the product, quantity and delivery location so we can confirm pricing and availability.

How can I become a dealer or distributor?+

Explore our partnership options, then contact us with your business type, location and preferred sales channel. Our team will discuss the available arrangements.
