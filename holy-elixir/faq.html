Frequently asked

The collection

What is in the Zubrex collection?+

Explore alkaline water, Black Drink and water ionizers. Our collection guide explains the differences.

What is Black Drink?+

The supplied Zubrex Black Drink pack describes it as a non-carbonated water based beverage and shows a 500 ml net quantity. See the product page for the actual pack photograph.

How do I check ingredients or storage directions?+

Read the current physical label. If you need information before ordering, ask our team for a current label image. Our label guide explains what to look for.

Frequently asked

Availability & enquiries

Can I buy directly on this website?+

This website helps you explore products and prepare an enquiry. There is no online checkout or payment collection. The team needs to confirm availability, price and order arrangements directly.

Do you deliver to my city?+

Share your city, postcode, product and quantity in an enquiry. Service coverage and delivery arrangements need to be confirmed before an order.

Can I request a repeat supply?+

Tell the team your preferred product, quantity and frequency. Any schedule, price and fulfilment arrangements require direct confirmation; this website does not start a subscription.

What happens when I use the enquiry form?+

The form prepares a draft in your email app. You review and send that email yourself. If no app opens, write directly to Info@holy-elixir.com.

Home & business

How do I choose an ionizer?+

Start with source-water suitability, installation requirements and service support for the exact model. Use the ionizer consultation checklist before requesting a quotation.

Can restaurants, hotels or event planners enquire?+

Yes. Visit hospitality & events for an enquiry checklist, or contact us with your venue, city, date and expected quantity.

How do I discuss distribution?+

Visit partnerships and share your business profile, territory and channels. Commercial terms and territory availability are discussed directly.
