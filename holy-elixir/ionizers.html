Your questions.

A useful appliance starts with a suitable setup. Before choosing an ionizer, discuss your incoming water quality, available connections, counter space and the model’s operating requirements.

Ask for the current specification sheet and a clear explanation of what is included. Model functions, filter arrangements and installation requirements can differ.

Ask before you choose

Useful questions, clear answers.

Does an ionizer replace every other water treatment system?+

Do not assume it does. Water suitability and any required treatment depend on the source water and the specific appliance. Ask for the manufacturer’s documented requirements.

Are all settings intended for drinking?+

Use only the settings designated for drinking in the current model’s manual. Follow its operating, cleaning and storage instructions.

How do I confirm the price and warranty?+

Request a model-specific quotation covering the appliance, installation, consumables, warranty and service. Confirm these details before making a purchase.
