Why Partner With Us?

Holy Elixir is more than a beverage — it’s a lifestyle brand anchored in science and luxury. As a partner, you will represent a product line that appeals to wellness enthusiasts, premium consumers and discerning businesses.

  • Rapidly growing market for alkaline and functional beverages.
  • Exclusive territories and attractive profit margins.
  • Comprehensive marketing, training and technical support.
  • Sustainable sourcing and eco‑friendly packaging that resonates with conscious consumers.
  • Backed by science and delivered with luxury aesthetics.

Who Can Join?

Our partner program is open to a wide range of professionals and enterprises who share our passion for wellness and luxury. Choose the model that fits your channel & geography:

Stockists

Perfect for premium retail shelves—health stores, gourmet retailers, boutique supermarkets, pharmacies and wellness studios.

  • Starter packs & flexible reorder quantities
  • Point‑of‑sale & merchandising support
  • Fast replenishment and seasonal bundles

Distributors

For partners serving multiple retail outlets across a city/region with consistent supply and account management.

  • Territory‑based pricing & volume slabs
  • Co‑marketing assets and product training
  • Support for retail onboarding and reorders

HoReCa Distributors

Hospitality & food‑service distribution for hotels, restaurants, cafés, resorts, spas, gyms and corporate cafeterias.

  • Menu‑friendly SKUs and event/hospitality packs
  • Consignment/rotation options where applicable
  • Brand storytelling collateral for guest experiences

Digital Marketplace

Ideal for e‑commerce stores, quick‑commerce and curated marketplaces focused on health and premium lifestyle categories.

  • High‑quality product images & listing copy
  • Bundle pricing and subscription‑ready packs
  • Marketplace compliance & fulfillment coordination

What we’ll ask for

  • City/territory coverage and channel type
  • Expected monthly volume (starter estimates are fine)
  • Business registration details (GST, license, etc. where applicable)
  • Delivery and payment preferences
