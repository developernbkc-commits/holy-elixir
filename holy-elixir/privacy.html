Website information

Your privacy.

How this website handles browsing and enquiry information.

Updated 28 October 2026

Browsing this website

The site serves its fonts, images, styles and scripts locally. It does not use advertising pixels, embedded social feeds, analytics scripts or tracking cookies in the current version. Our hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

Making an enquiry

The enquiry form prepares an email draft on your device. It does not submit the form to a website database. The information reaches Holy Elixir when you send that email through your chosen email service. You can also call or email us directly.

What to include

Share the contact details and product or business information needed to answer your question. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for an enquiry.

Other websites

If you follow an external educational link, that destination operates under its own privacy practices. Email and telephone links use the applications you choose on your device.

Questions about your information

Contact Info@holy-elixir.com to ask about enquiry information you have sent or to request a correction. Include enough context to identify the enquiry.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the website’s information-handling features change. The date above identifies this version.
