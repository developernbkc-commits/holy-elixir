The product notes / Reading your label

Clarity starts
with the pack.

The product in your hand carries the details that matter. Use this guide to know where to look and what to ask.

A 3-minute read · Choosing with confidence

A practical reading guide

Six things to look for.

01

Product identity

Start with the product name and declared category. A Black Drink beverage and packaged alkaline water are distinct product choices.

02

Ingredients & nutrition

Read the complete list and nutritional declaration where provided. For a particular ingredient question, share a clear label photo with our team.

03

Net quantity

Check the stated volume and number of packs in the offer. Confirm the current format when asking for a quotation.

04

Batch & date details

Locate the batch identification and date markings. These details help the team identify the product if you have a query.

05

Storage & use

Follow the directions on the current pack, including after-opening instructions. Keep the seal intact until you are ready to use the product.

06

Manufacturer & contact details

Use the details printed on the pack for product-specific queries. Keep your invoice and packaging information when asking for support.
