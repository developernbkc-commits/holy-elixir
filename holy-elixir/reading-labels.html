The pack, explained

Six details in focus.

01

The identity

Look for the Zubrex Black Drink name and the declared beverage category.

02

Composition & nutrition

The current ingredient list and nutritional declaration carry the product-specific details.

03

The format

Check the declared volume and number of packs in the offer. The supplied signature format is 500 ml.

04

Batch & date

The batch identification and date markings help identify the pack and its intended use period.

05

Storage & serving

Follow the current pack’s storage, serving and after-opening directions.

06

Your point of contact

Manufacturer and contact details are printed on the pack. Keep the batch details if you need product support.
