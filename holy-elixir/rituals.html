01 / The visual signature

The camera
loves contrast.

A dark drink in a clear glass has a graphic quality all its own. Against pale stone, linen or a sunlit background, the contrast becomes the detail that catches the eye.

For your own table or a photograph of the occasion, leave room around the glass. A natural backdrop and a single considered detail are often all you need.

Explore the design story↗

02 / At the table

Good company
sets the scene.

A long lunch does not need a complicated setting. Set out comfortable glassware, make room for shared plates and let the conversation set the pace.

Bring Black Drink to the table as a detail in the gathering. Serve it in the way that suits the moment, with the pack nearby for anyone who wants a closer look.

Read the serving notes↗

03 / A change of scene

An open afternoon.
A fresh perspective.

A courtyard, a terrace, a spot in the shade. Taking a familiar moment outside can give it a completely different feel.

Bring clean glasses, a simple arrangement and a little time. Follow the pack’s storage directions while travelling and take used packaging with you.
