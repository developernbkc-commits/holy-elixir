The science notes

A little science.
A clearer picture.

Composition, pH, alkalinity and ionization describe different things. A short guide to the language around water-based drinks.

A 2-minute read · The essentials

Composition & character

There is detail in the composition.

The type and concentration of dissolved minerals can influence taste. The full formulation and the amounts present matter; colour alone does not reveal a drink’s composition.

01

What is included

An ingredient declaration identifies the substances used in a particular product. Read the complete current list for the product in your hand.

02

How much is present

A quantity needs a unit and a reference volume. Figures per 100 ml and per serving describe different amounts.

03

The whole picture

An ingredient name, appearance or single measurement is only one part of a product’s description. Read the details together.

Three terms, clearly explained

Different properties.
Different meanings.

pH

Acidity & basicity

pH describes how acidic or basic an aqueous solution is. A pH value is a measurement of that property.

A

Alkalinity

Alkalinity describes the capacity of a solution to neutralize added acid. It is a different measurement from pH.

±

Ionization

Ionization is the formation of electrically charged atoms or molecules, called ions. The term describes a chemical process.

These are general explanations of the terms. The current Black Drink label is the place to check its product declarations.
