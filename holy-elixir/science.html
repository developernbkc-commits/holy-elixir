A Little Clarity

Understand What’s in Your Glass

Straightforward explanations of pH, minerals and ionization. No complicated promises.

01 / pH

What Does Alkaline Mean?

pH describes how acidic or alkaline a liquid is. A pH of 7 is neutral; values above 7 are alkaline. Check the product label for the stated pH of your water.

A higher pH is a product characteristic. It does not establish a health benefit.

02 / Ingredients

Black Drink & Trace Minerals

Holy Elixir Black Drink is a beverage with fulvic acid and trace minerals. The full ingredient list and nutrition information on the pack are the best reference for what is in each serving.

Ingredient presence alone does not establish a detox, immunity or treatment benefit.

03 / Technology

How Ionization Works

Water ionizers use electrolysis to produce streams with different pH levels. Filtration is a separate part of the system; performance depends on the model, source water and maintenance.

Use only the settings designated for drinking in your model’s instructions.

04 / Measurements

Understanding ORP

Oxidation–reduction potential (ORP) is a measurement expressed in millivolts. It describes a liquid’s tendency to accept or donate electrons under the measurement conditions.

An ORP reading by itself does not demonstrate a health effect after drinking a beverage.

Keep the Evidence in Perspective

Research into alkaline water continues, but findings about one formulation or study cannot automatically be applied to another product. We focus on product characteristics and clear label information.

Read Mayo Clinic’s overview of alkaline water for an independent explanation of the evidence.
