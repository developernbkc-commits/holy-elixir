The journal / Serving notes

The art of
a simple serve.

A thoughtful table is made of small details. Here are a few to consider when serving the collection.

A 3-minute read · Hosting & everyday moments

Begin with the basics

A clear glass.
A little intention.

Clean, clear glassware lets the drink’s appearance take the lead. For a shared table, set out glasses before guests arrive and keep the product pack nearby so its label is easy to check.

Follow the pack’s directions for storage and serving temperature. Check the seal and date before opening, and use the opened product as directed on the label.

A hosting checklist

Four details worth considering.

  1. Give every guest a clear choice.

    Let guests know which product is being served. Keep ingredient and product information available rather than guessing when someone asks.

  2. Keep the setting simple.

    A light tablecloth, uncluttered glassware and a few natural details can create an inviting setting without competing with the drink.

  3. Serve with care.

    Use clean glasses and utensils. Follow the current pack’s storage and after-opening instructions, including any stated time limits.

  4. Plan the quantity.

    For a gathering or venue, share the guest count and date when enquiring. Confirm pack formats, availability and delivery arrangements in advance.
